How to play

Best with 4–8 players. One of you is secretly the spy.

  1. Secret roles. Everyone sees the same location and their own role. The spy sees neither — just a blank.
  2. Ask questions. Take turns asking things like "How often do you come here?" Answer vaguely: prove you belong without naming the place.
  3. Spot the spy. Someone too vague — or oddly specific — is suspicious. Tap players to privately mark your suspects.
  4. Reveal. When the group is ready, the host reveals all. Non-spies win by catching the spy; the spy wins by guessing the location.
